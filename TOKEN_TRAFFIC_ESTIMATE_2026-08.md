# $FIRECHESS Pump.fun traffic estimate

**Checked:** 6 August 2026. These are planning ranges, not promised outcomes.

## Evidence

- The [RED-PUMP-2026-v1 dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/20633487) observed 860,213 Pump.fun launches over 34 days (about 25,300 per day or 18 per minute). Of 832,941 launches with a 24-hour terminal outcome, 1,651 graduated: 0.198%, or about 1 in 505.
- The same dataset reports an 8.94x graduation-rate association for launches advertising Telegram, but this is correlation, not proof that Telegram causes success, and it does not measure outbound website clicks.
- [FireChess's public stats endpoint](https://www.firechess.com/api/stats) reported 632 users, 165 reports, 4 Pro members and 8 lifetime members when checked. The 12 paid/lifetime users equal about 1.9% of registered users, but Pump.fun referrals are likely less qualified than existing FireChess acquisition sources.
- Pump.fun does not publish a first-party benchmark for coin-page impressions, outbound website click-through, FireChess signups or paid conversion. Those parts of the scenarios below are explicit assumptions.

## Planning scenarios for the first 7 days

| Scenario | Incremental site visits | New accounts | New paid users | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Launch only, roughly $5 and no outside audience | 0-20 | 0-2 | 0 | Most likely outcome |
| Coordinated memes, clips, X/Telegram posting and a clear FireChess landing page | 30-200 | 3-20 | 0-1 | The content campaign, not token creation, produces most traffic |
| Rare breakout | 1,000+ | 50+ | Highly variable | Outlier, not a forecast; graduation itself occurred in only 0.198% of measured launches |

These ranges assume approximately 2-8% of people who seriously inspect the coin click the site and 5-15% of those visitors create an account. Neither conversion rate is measured by Pump.fun. A token launch would create referral/social traffic, not automatically improve FireChess's search rankings.

## Recommendation

Do not launch $FIRECHESS primarily as a traffic-acquisition tactic. A seven-day Chaos Chess meme challenge or short-video campaign is more likely to attract qualified chess users. If a token experiment is run anyway, use tagged links, a dedicated landing page, and success criteria based on unique visits, completed scans and registrations rather than token price or trading volume.
