# Research Brief: Chess Tactics Every Player Should Know

## Topic
Comprehensive guide to essential chess tactical patterns — the must-know weapons every chess player needs.

## Why This Topic
- **Priority Queue #7**: next item in the pipeline
- **Search potential**: "chess tactics" is a high-volume evergreen keyword; "chess tactics for beginners" "essential chess tactics" "types of chess tactics" all have strong monthly search volume
- **Competition**: Chess.com, Lichess, and The Spruce Crafts all rank for variants, but there's room for a FireChess-specific guide with interactive chess-position boards and internal links to FireChess tools
- **Internal linking opportunity**: Can link to /puzzles, /analysis, /dungeon, /glossary pages

## Target Audience
- Beginners to intermediate (400-1500 Elo)
- Looking to improve tactical awareness
- Need clear examples with visual boards

## Key Patterns to Cover
1. Fork (knight fork, pawn fork)
2. Pin (absolute, relative)
3. Skewer
4. Discovered Attack & Double Check
5. Zwischenzug (in-between move)
6. Removing the Defender
7. Back Rank Mate
8. Deflection & Overloaded Pieces

## Structure
- Introduction: tactics win games (80%+ of amateur games decided by tactics)
- 8 main sections, each with FEN position examples
- Training plan section with internal links
- Conclusion with actionable next steps
