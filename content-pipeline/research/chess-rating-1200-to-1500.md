- Priority queue item #5 (next in queue)

- 1200-1500 is the most populous rating bracket; every player goes through this transition

## Key Points
1. 1200-1300: biggest problem is hanging pieces and 1-move blunders
2. 1300-1400: tactical awareness improves but positional understanding is weak
3. 1400-1500: endgames and strategy become the differentiator

## Core Pillars
1. **Blunder elimination** — build the pre-move checklist habit
2. **Opening repertoire** — not more openings, but the right ones
3. **Endgame fundamentals** — convert won positions, save drawn ones
4. **Middlegame thinking** — finding a plan, not just reacting
