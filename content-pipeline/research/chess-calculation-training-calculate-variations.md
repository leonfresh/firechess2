# Research Brief: Chess Calculation Training

## Topic
**How to calculate chess variations deeper** — Priority queue #10, Cycle 11

## Search Landscape
- High-intent queries: "chess calculation training", "how to calculate chess variations", "improve chess calculation", "visualization in chess"
- Competitor coverage: Chess.com has lessons on calculation; Lichess has training tools; YouTube has endless GM calculation tutorials (Dany, Hanging Pawns, GothamChess)
- Gap in the market: Most calculation guides are either too abstract (GM advice you can't apply) or too specific (one-off tactics). Few bridge the gap with *actionable frameworks* a 1200-1800 player can practice daily.

## Content Strategy
- **Primary angle**: Teach **four concrete calculation methods** (candidate moves, forcing moves first, tree-of-variations, blunder check) — each with a chess-position diagram, a worked example, and a practice drill
- **Secondary angle**: Frame calculation as a *learnable skill*, not a talent — use research on deliberate practice and pattern recognition
- **Tertiary angle**: Connect to FireChess's analysis board as the ideal calculation training tool

## Target Keywords
- Primary: "chess calculation training" (moderate volume, high intent)
- Secondary: "calculate chess variations", "how to improve chess calculation", "chess visualization exercises", "candidate moves chess"

## Article Structure
1. **Why calculation separates the levels** — the gap between guessing and calculating
2. **Method 1: Generate candidate moves** — systematic list-making (with diagram)
3. **Method 2: Forcing moves first** — checks, captures, threats (with diagram)
4. **Method 3: The tree of variations** — depth-first, not breadth-first (with diagram)
5. **Method 4: The blunder check** — what you missed
6. **Visualization exercises** — seeing without moving pieces
7. **Time management for calculators** — when to go deep, when to trust intuition
8. **A 30-day calculation training routine**
9. **Comparison table: calculation methods**
10. **Conclusion: Train with FireChess**
