In [Chaos Chess](/chaos), you draft a permanent piece modifier every 5 turns, and the picks you make decide the game far more than your opening does. New to the mode? Start with [how to play Chaos Chess](/blog/how-to-play-chaos-chess) first — this post assumes you know the draft loop and just want to know **what to pick.**

After a *lot* of games, here's how the modifiers actually shake out. Tiers are about win impact, not rarity — some "common" picks punch way above their tier, and some legendaries are flashy traps.

## S-Tier — pick these almost every time

| Modifier | Tier (in-game) | Why it's broken |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🔫 **Queen Cannon** | Epic | Your Queen leaps one piece to capture behind it, always on. Pins stop existing, blockades collapse, and the threat range is suffocating. |
| 👸 **The Amazon** | Legendary | Queen + Knight movement on one piece. It forks the king and a rook from squares a normal queen can't touch. Often just wins on the spot. |
| 🏇 **The Knook** | Rare | Knight + Rook on one piece, available *early*. A rare that warps the board from phase 2 — absurd value for how soon you can get it. |
| ⚡ **Railgun** | Legendary | Once per game your rook fires down all four lines at once, piercing blockers. A guaranteed multi-capture you fire at the perfect moment. |

These share a trait: they add *threats your opponent can't model with normal chess intuition.* That's what wins Chaos Chess.

## A-Tier — strong, situational, rarely wrong

| Modifier | Tier | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ☢️ **Nuclear Queen** | Legendary | Clears all 8 squares around a queen capture. Devastating in a crowded center — but the 3-turn cooldown and "doesn't kill the king" caveat keep it out of S. |
| 🌙 **Night Rider** | Rare | Chained L-jumps make a single knight cover the whole board. Brutal pressure if you have open lines. |
| 🔱 **King Ascension** | Legendary | Your king captures like a queen. A defensive monster that turns your king into an attacker — just remember it can still be mated. |
| 🐎 **Knight Horde** | Legendary | Two free knights. Raw material that snowballs, though placement is random. |
| 💥 **Collateral Damage** | Epic | Rook captures also kill the piece behind the target. Trades two-for-one all game. |

## B-Tier — good in the right build

| Modifier | Tier | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🚀 **Torpedo Pawns** | Common | Two-square pawn moves from any rank. Quietly one of the best *commons* — it combos hard with pawn-respawn picks into an endless tide. |
| 🐉 **Dragon Bishop** | Common | One orthogonal step frees your bishop from its color forever. Reliable, never dead. |
| 💀 **Undead Army** | Legendary | Resurrects all your missing pawns. Game-swinging *if* you've lost pawns — a dead pick if you haven't. |
| 👻 **Phantom Rook** | Rare | Rooks slide through your own pieces. Unlocks your rooks early without trading. |
| 🧨 **Kamikaze Bishop** | Legendary | Guaranteed trade when your bishop dies. Great value, but you're betting on losing the bishop. |

## C-Tier — flashy traps and niche picks

- **🌀 Warp Queen** *(epic)* — one teleport per game looks amazing, but a single repositioning rarely changes the result. You usually want continuous pressure, not a one-time hop.
- **🪃 Ricochet Bishop** *(epic)* — edge-bouncing is hard to calculate *for you* too. Cool, inconsistent.
- **⛓️ King's Chains** *(common)* — paralyzing one adjacent enemy piece is fine, but you rarely want your king near enemy pieces in the first place.

## The real secret: draft for synergy, not stars

The single biggest mistake players make is grabbing the rarest card on offer. Chaos Chess rewards **combos over rarity**:

- **Torpedo Pawns + Pawn Fortress** → a self-replenishing pawn avalanche that just walks up the board.
- **Any Cannon piece (Queen/Bishop/Rook Cannon) + a crowded center** → you turn the opponent's own pieces into launch pads.
- **Knook or Amazon + open files** → a single super-piece that creates threats faster than the opponent can defend.

And remember the part everyone forgets: **it's still chess underneath.** A B-tier pick that fits your position beats an S-tier pick that doesn't. Tempo, king safety, and development decide more games than any single modifier.
